The daily special sells **hero shards twice a day**. This calculator compares
the two ways to pay — **voucher packs** and **gold blocks** bought with the
weekly +110% bundles — and finds the cheapest combination for your target.



## How the daily special works

- Each day you can buy the daily special **twice**: the first buy gives
  **10 shards**, the second gives **30 more** — 40 shards per day in total.
- Every buy can be paid with **vouchers** or **gold blocks**. You can mix
  them freely, e.g. vouchers for the first buy and gold for the second.
- Vouchers come from the **Daily Deal Activation Pack** — Rp 60.000 (discounted
  from Rp 93.000). It unlocks the [Daily Duel] pack in the in-game shop and
  contains **Daily Special Activation Voucher ×6**. Leftover vouchers carry
  over to the next day, so nothing is wasted.
- Paying with vouchers earns **more star gems** than paying with gold.

## Costs side by side

|                        | Tier 1 — 10 shards        | Tier 2 — +30 shards     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| With vouchers          | 6 vouchers (Rp 60.000)    | 20 vouchers (≈ Rp 200.000) |
| With gold blocks       | 93.000 gold (≈ Rp 44.286) | 310.000 gold (≈ Rp 147.619) |
| Star gems — vouchers / gold | 50 / 35              | 150 / 105               |

The Rupiah figures for gold assume you bought it with the weekly **+110%
bundles** — that is what makes gold cheaper per shard than vouchers. Vouchers
still earn more star gems, which is the trade-off the calculator makes for
you.

## Weekly gold bundles

Every bundle gives a **+110% bonus** — pay Rp 77.000, receive 161.700 gold:

| Bundle | Price      | Total gold | Per week |
| ------ | ---------- | ---------- | -------- |
| Small  | Rp 77.000  | 161.700    | 2        |
| Medium | Rp 155.000 | 325.500    | 1        |
| Large  | Rp 310.000 | 651.000    | 1        |

All bundles together give **1.299.900 gold per week** — enough for about 3
days of daily specials bought fully with gold. For the rest of the week the
calculator fills in with vouchers, because topping up gold beyond the bundles
(paying 1 Rupiah per gold without bonus) always costs more than vouchers.
